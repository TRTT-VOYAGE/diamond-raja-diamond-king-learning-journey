# Journal Rules

1. Publish curated learning, not raw conversation transcripts.
2. Keep public-facing writing in English for broad accessibility.
3. Record the date, context, decisions, lessons, evidence, and next steps.
4. Distinguish verified outcomes from assumptions, proposals, and untested ideas.
5. Correct mistakes transparently; do not silently rewrite history.
6. Keep each daily chapter focused and useful. Avoid filler or repetitive chat.
7. Attribute AI-assisted work honestly; Diamond King is an AI assistant, not a human account holder or legal person.
8. Do not publish private messages from other people without their consent.
9. Link to public project evidence when useful, but never expose secrets or private repository contents.
10. A daily chapter is prepared only when enough reliable context is available. No fabricated activity or automatic-chat-sync claims.
