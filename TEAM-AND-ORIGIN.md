## AI collaborator

**Diamond King — ChatGPT**

Diamond King is the project name used for the AI assistant supporting this work. Its contributions may include research assistance, outlining, drafting, code suggestions, technical analysis, documentation, and verification planning.

Diamond King is an AI system, not a human being, legal founder, employee, account holder, or independent rights owner. Its output can be incomplete or wrong and must be reviewed. It cannot independently access every private chat or operate continuously outside available tools and permissions.

## Why this project began

The project began from the desire to preserve the useful knowledge created while a human builder works with an AI assistant. A simple diary was not enough: the long-term ambition is to explore whether the same workflow can become a practical product for other people and teams.

The central question is:

> How might people preserve the best outcomes of human–AI work as trustworthy, searchable, portable project knowledge—without turning private conversations into public data?
