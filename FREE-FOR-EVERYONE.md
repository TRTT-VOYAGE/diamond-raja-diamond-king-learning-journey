# Diamond King Learning Journey — Free for Everyone 👑💎

**Learn → Imagine → Build → Share**

> Go take what you need, build your own ship!

## Our public promise

The Diamond King Learning Journey is a public learning resource. Its purpose is to make useful knowledge, practical lessons, ideas, and build-in-public examples accessible to everyone.

Take what is useful. Learn at your own pace. Imagine what could exist. Build your own projects. Share what you learn so others can build too.

## Four-step learning loop

1. **Learn** — explore concepts, tools, examples, and lessons.
2. **Imagine** — connect ideas and define a problem worth solving.
3. **Build** — turn learning into experiments, projects, and useful work.
4. **Share** — document outcomes, evidence, mistakes, and reusable knowledge.

## Open-access principles

- Public learning content is intended to be free for everyone.
- No paid membership is required to read this public learning journey.
- Reuse and licensing remain subject to each source's licence, attribution, privacy, and third-party rights; “free” does not mean every asset is public domain.
- Never share private conversations, credentials, personal data, or confidential material.
- Clearly distinguish verified results, illustrative examples, experiments, and future plans.
- This repository is a curated public learning resource, not a deployed SaaS product or a promise of commercial services.
